The real-world problem

Why ordinary waste disposal creates a chain of problems.

1

Mixed waste

Recyclable and non-recyclable items end up in the same bin.

2

Low awareness

People may not know the right category or disposal method.

3

Contamination

Wrongly sorted items reduce the value of recyclables.

4

Slow sorting

Manual sorting is repetitive, slow and hard to scale.

The key gap: show a waste photo, get an AI answer and a practical disposal tip.

Limitations and responsible use

What should be improved before large-scale use.

Training data matters

The AI is only as good as the photos it learned from.

Real-world conditions

Poor light, odd angles, dirty or mixed items can confuse it.

Confidence ≠ certainty

A high score does not guarantee a correct answer.

Data and privacy

Uploaded images and records need safe storage and access control.

Local rules

A city rollout needs more categories and local disposal rules.

When the app is not sure, it says “uncertain” instead of guessing.
